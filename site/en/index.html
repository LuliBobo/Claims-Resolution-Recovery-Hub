A complaint usually breaks into five small jobs

In a small team one person handles them between inboxes and spreadsheets: read the message, find the order and shipment, check the photos, find the rule, write to the customer and then separately write to the carrier. The last step is easy to lose. The carrier claim is never filed because one photo was missing or the deadline passed, and the loss stays with you.

How it works on one case

Example case: a Spanish customer reports a broken glass vase. All screenshots come from the demo mode.

1. Understanding the case

The message is read and classified

The system detects the language, writes an internal English summary, sets the case type and links the order and shipment.

You can correct the summary and the type. Only reviewers can change the type and priority, because the evidence check depends on them.

2. Evidence check

A missing photo is found before the claim is filed

For a damaged parcel the carrier requires three things: the shipping label, the damaged item and the outer carton. The checklist is recomputed from the current attachments on every view.

The AI only describes what is in an attachment. Whether the evidence is sufficient is decided by a fixed rule, not by the model's impression. A request for the missing photo is drafted in the customer's language. You send it yourself.

3. Backed by your rules

The recommendation shows where it comes from

The system searches your PDF documents for relevant passages and stores them verbatim, with the document name, version and page. That makes the recommendation checkable.

While a photo is missing, the proposal is "Request Missing Evidence". Once it arrives a new proposal is created and the old one stays in the history as superseded.

4. Workflow score

Who should see the case, and how fast

Five factors from 0 to 3 (evidence completeness, policy clarity, customer impact, business exposure, recovery potential) give a total score and a suggested path, from a quick review to escalation with no automatic closure.

The score is recomputed from current data. A reviewer can override it, but must give a reason, and the override is audited. The thresholds are prototype working assumptions, not a legal standard.

5. Claim to the carrier

The same complaint produces a second outcome

The system drafts the claim: who it is addressed to, which shipment it concerns, the reason and an estimate of the recoverable amount.

Damaged and missing items are claimed from the carrier, other cases from the supplier. Only one open claim draft can exist per case, so the same loss is not claimed twice.

6. Approval and history

A person decides, everything is recorded

A reviewer can approve, approve with edits, request more evidence or reject. Edits are stored as the original and the new value.

The system sends nothing. You confirm sending manually, and every step is written to a history that cannot be changed afterwards.

What the tool does and does not do

It does

  • Prepares the groundwork: summary, list of missing evidence, proposed resolution, text for the customer and a draft claim.
  • Shows which of your documents a recommendation is based on.
  • Records approvals, edits and reasons in a history that cannot be changed.
  • Helps you recover money from carriers by checking evidence in time.

It does not

  • Send emails, claims or refunds. A person always sends them.
  • Decide whether evidence is sufficient on the model's judgement.
  • Give legal advice. The score and its thresholds are working assumptions.
  • Read scanned PDFs without a text layer (there is no OCR).
  • Replace a helpdesk. It is an add-on for complaints and recovery.

Project status

A prototype looking for its first pilot

The application is a working prototype. It is covered by automated tests (unit, integration against a real database and end-to-end in a browser).

The screenshots on this page come from the demo mode with invented data. The attachments are generated placeholder images and the policies are sample texts.

We have not yet run the AI steps against a live model. In the demo mode and in the tests they are replaced by prepared answers, so we make no claims yet about output quality on your real complaints.

We have not tried a production deployment yet; it is prepared and awaiting verification.
