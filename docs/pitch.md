# Pitch

## Tagline

**Resolve faster. Recover more. Keep humans in control.**

## 30-second version

Small e-commerce teams often handle returns and damaged-goods complaints through inboxes, spreadsheets and manual follow-up. Claims Resolution & Recovery Hub turns one complaint into a controlled workflow: it classifies the case, checks missing evidence, retrieves the right internal policy, recommends a resolution, drafts the customer reply and prepares a recovery claim for the carrier or supplier. In our demo, one photo-based complaint becomes both a customer replacement decision and a carrier recovery package, with a human approving the final actions. The result is faster resolution, more consistent decisions and fewer unrecovered losses.

## Positioning

Not a white-space market — returns-management platforms, warranty tools and specialised claims software already exist. The differentiated hypothesis: a lightweight, multilingual, evidence-driven recovery layer for small/mid-sized merchants still running complaints through inboxes and spreadsheets, with a path from a Luo prototype to a standalone operations product.

## Main risks to watch

- Scope creep: this is not a full helpdesk/RMA/carrier-integration platform — keep the MVP to one or two complaint stories.
- Generic-AI-assistant feel: the demo must show evidence checks, policy grounding, recovery logic and approval, not just generated text.
- False legal certainty: never claim to give legal advice or determine statutory rights.
- Opaque decisions: every recommendation must show why it was made.
- Vendor lock-in: keep schemas, rules, prompts and demo data maintained here, not only inside Luo.
