# Claims Resolution & Recovery Hub — Branding and Naming Options

> **Decision:** the project name stays **"Claims Resolution & Recovery Hub"**. "ResolveLoop" (this document's recommendation) and "ClaimFlow" (used in `docs/claimflow-luo-playbook.md` and the workflow diagram, `docs/assets/claimflow-workflow-diagram.jpg`) were both considered and not adopted. This document is kept as source material for the naming/visual-identity options that were evaluated, not as the current brand.

## Brand direction
The product should feel:

- operational rather than playful,
- trustworthy rather than overpromising,
- international and easy to pronounce,
- useful for both customer support and finance/operations,
- credible in a hackathon and extensible into a SaaS product.

Avoid putting **AI**, **Returns** or **Claims** in the primary brand name if possible. Those words explain the category, but they make the name generic and place it too close to existing returns platforms. The market already contains products focused on returns, warranties, repairs, exchanges and supplier workflows, including Claimlane, Loop Returns and ReturnGO.[web:205][web:252]

Use a distinctive brand name, followed by a descriptive subtitle:

> **[Brand name] — Multilingual claims resolution and recovery for e-commerce.**

## Recommended name

# ResolveLoop

### Descriptor
**ResolveLoop — The complaint-to-recovery operating layer for e-commerce.**

### Tagline
**Resolve faster. Recover more. Keep humans in control.**

### Why it works
- “Resolve” describes the customer outcome.
- “Loop” suggests a closed operational loop from complaint to resolution to recovery to prevention.
- It is short and easy to say in English.
- It does not lock the product into only returns; it can later include warranty, delivery damage, supplier quality and post-purchase issues.
- It fits a hackathon pitch and a future B2B SaaS product.

### Naming risk
“Loop” is common in technology and e-commerce branding, so trademark and domain availability must be verified before public use. The name is a working recommendation, not a confirmed available trademark or domain.

## Recommended visual identity

### Logo concept
Use a simple circular or semi-circular mark containing two connected paths:

- an outward path representing the customer resolution,
- a returning path representing recovery from the carrier or supplier.

Avoid using a shopping cart, package icon or robot head. Those symbols make the product look like a generic e-commerce or AI tool.

### Icon concept
A minimal loop made from two arrows around a small evidence dot:

- evidence enters,
- a decision is made,
- the case resolves,
- value returns to the merchant.

### Colour palette
- **Deep navy:** `#102A43` — trust, operations and reliability.
- **Teal:** `#168F8B` — resolution, progress and recovery.
- **Warm amber:** `#F2B84B` — human review and attention required.
- **Soft background:** `#F5F7FA` — clean workspace background.
- **Critical red:** `#D64545` — blocked, incomplete or escalated cases.

Use amber for “Needs review”, not red. Red should be reserved for actual escalation or blocked status.

### Typography
Use a clean sans-serif such as Inter, Geist, IBM Plex Sans or a similar readable interface font. Use sentence case in buttons and labels. Avoid excessive uppercase text.

## Product language

### Preferred terms
- Customer case
- Evidence check
- Policy support
- Resolution proposal
- Recovery draft
- Human review
- Recovery opportunity
- Root-cause insight
- Audit timeline

### Avoid
- AI decides
- Automatic legal decision
- Guaranteed refund outcome
- Fully autonomous claims processing
- Zero-touch resolution
- Legal compliance engine

The product should present AI as structured decision support, not as an authority that determines customer rights.

## Status language

- New
- Under Review
- Waiting for Evidence
- Policy Check Complete
- Resolution Proposed
- Waiting for Approval
- Recovery Draft Ready
- Approved
- Rejected

## Recommended pitch wording

### One-line description
> ResolveLoop turns multilingual e-commerce complaints into evidence-backed customer resolutions and carrier or supplier recovery actions, with human approval built in.

### Short tagline
> One complaint in. Two controlled outcomes out.

### 30-second brand version
> ResolveLoop is a multilingual complaint-to-recovery operating layer for small e-commerce teams. It detects the customer’s language, checks evidence, retrieves internal policy, recommends a resolution, drafts the customer reply and prepares a carrier or supplier recovery action. Human reviewers approve important outcomes, and every step is recorded. ResolveLoop helps merchants resolve faster, recover more and learn from recurring operational failures.

## Naming validation checklist
Before publishing the name, check:

- domain availability for the preferred extension,
- exact-match web search,
- EUIPO trademark search,
- national trademark databases for target markets,
- GitHub organisation and repository availability,
- social-media handle availability,
- potential confusion with established returns products,
- pronunciation in English and the user’s target languages.

This document does not confirm trademark, domain or company-name availability. Treat all proposed names as working concepts until a proper legal and availability check is completed.

## Final recommendation
Use **ResolveLoop** as the working brand and **One Complaint, Two Controlled Outcomes** as the hackathon headline.

Recommended presentation:

> **ResolveLoop**  
> *One Complaint, Two Controlled Outcomes*  
> Multilingual claims resolution and recovery for e-commerce.

This gives the project a distinctive identity while preserving an immediately understandable explanation of what the product does.
