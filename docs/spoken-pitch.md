## Main spoken pitch (about 2.5 minutes)
Small e-commerce teams still handle returns, damaged deliveries and complaint cases through shared inboxes, spreadsheets and manual follow-up. That creates delays, inconsistent decisions and a hidden financial problem: even when the customer gets a resolution, the business often fails to recover the loss from the carrier or supplier.

That is the problem we are solving with **Claims Resolution & Recovery Hub**.

Our product is a multilingual complaint-to-recovery workflow for small and mid-sized e-commerce teams. It takes a messy customer complaint and turns it into two controlled outcomes: first, a clear customer resolution, and second, a recovery action against the responsible carrier or supplier.

Here is how it works.

A customer sends a complaint in their own language, for example Slovak, and uploads photos of a broken product. The system detects the language, creates an internal English summary, classifies the issue, links it to the original order and shipment, and checks whether the evidence is complete. It then retrieves the relevant internal policy, recommends the next action, drafts a customer reply in the original language and prepares a recovery draft for the carrier or supplier.

But the important point is this: the system does not act like a black box and it does not remove human control. Every meaningful action can be reviewed, edited or rejected by a human operator. That makes the workflow faster without making it reckless.

What makes this product different is that it does not stop at customer support. Most tools help merchants answer faster. We help them answer faster **and** recover losses more consistently. That means better customer experience, fewer manual steps, more explainable decisions and stronger margin protection.

In our flagship demo, a complaint about a broken vase becomes both a replacement decision for the customer and a carrier recovery draft in under one minute. The system then records the full audit trail and updates analytics to show repeated issues by SKU, supplier or carrier.

So this is not just an AI writing tool. It is an operational layer for complaint handling, recovery and prevention.

**Claims Resolution & Recovery Hub turns one complaint into one customer resolution and one recovery action, with AI assistance and human approval built in.**

## Short spoken pitch (about 60 seconds)
Small e-commerce teams still handle complaints through inboxes, spreadsheets and manual follow-up. That is slow, inconsistent and expensive, especially when damaged deliveries or supplier errors create losses that never get recovered.

Claims Resolution & Recovery Hub is a multilingual workflow that turns one complaint into two controlled outcomes: a customer resolution and a recovery action against the carrier or supplier.

The system detects the customer language, summarises the case in English, classifies the issue, checks missing evidence, retrieves the relevant policy, recommends the next step, drafts the customer reply and prepares the recovery draft. Then a human reviews the important actions before anything moves forward.

What makes this different is simple: we are not just helping merchants answer complaints faster. We are helping them resolve faster, recover more and keep humans in control.

## Delivery notes
- Speak more slowly than you think you need to.
- Pause after the phrase “two controlled outcomes”.
- Emphasise the difference between customer resolution and business recovery.
- Do not overuse the word AI.
- Say “human approval” clearly, because it increases trust.
- If time is short, skip the analytics sentence before the closing line.
