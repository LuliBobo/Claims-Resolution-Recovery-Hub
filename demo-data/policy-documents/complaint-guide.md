# Merchant Complaint Guide (sample, v1.2)

> Sample internal policy document used by the Policy Retrieval Agent demo. Not a real legal document.

## Section 3 — Shipping damage claims

3.1 A shipping-damage complaint requires: (a) a photo of the damaged item, and (b) a photo of the outer packaging as received. Without the outer-packaging photo, the claim is classified as evidence-incomplete and a customer request for the missing photo must be sent before a resolution is proposed.

3.2 For fragile-goods categories (glass, ceramics), a confirmed shipping-damage complaint with complete evidence is eligible for immediate replacement, pending stock availability, without waiting for the carrier claim outcome.

3.3 Every confirmed shipping-damage case must generate a carrier recovery claim referencing the same evidence used for the customer resolution.

## Section 5 — Wrong-item complaints

5.1 A wrong-item complaint requires: (a) a photo of the item received, and (b) the order confirmation or invoice showing the item ordered.

5.2 Confirmed wrong-item cases are resolved by shipping the correct item and issuing a prepaid return label for the incorrect item; a full refund is offered only if the correct item is out of stock.

5.3 Repeated wrong-item cases against the same SKU within a rolling 30-day window must be flagged for supplier or warehouse review.
