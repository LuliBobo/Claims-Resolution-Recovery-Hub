# Carrier Claim Rules — NordicShip Parcel Services (sample, v2.0)

> Sample carrier policy excerpt used by the Recovery Agent demo. Not a real legal document.

## Damage claims

- Claim must be filed within 14 calendar days of the delivery date.
- Required evidence: photo of the damaged item, photo of the outer packaging (all sides), tracking ID, and the original order invoice.
- Maximum recoverable amount per parcel: declared value at shipment, capped at 200 EUR unless additional insurance was purchased.
- Claims missing the outer-packaging photo are auto-rejected by the carrier's own intake system.

## Loss claims

- Claim must be filed within 21 calendar days of the expected delivery date.
- Required evidence: tracking ID showing no final delivery scan, and the original order invoice.
